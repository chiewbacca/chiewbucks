How a party pops

Emily facilitates. Grown-ups host the house. Kids leave with a labelled pot and slime on their hands (it washes off — mostly).

1

Pick a package

Tell us the birthday kid’s name, how many friends, and whether you want fluffy, glitter, or glow.

2

Emily arrives ready

Aprons, bowls, safe recipes, and a mini science intro. Tables get a splash mat. Music goes on.

3

Mix, name, stretch

Every kid makes their own batch, invents a silly slime name, and votes on the stretchiest one.

4

Pot it and party on

Slime goes home in a sealed tub with a sticker. Cake time is yours. Emily packs the glitter away.

Hi, I’m Emily

I’m 12, I live around Cracroft, and I have strong opinions about slime texture. Too sticky is a crime. Too stiff is boring. The sweet spot jiggles.

At your party I run the workshop like a tiny lab host: welcome, recipe, mix, stretch test, stickers. Parents stay in the room. I bring washable, kid-tested recipes and I do not let glitter escape unsupervised. (It tries.)

Where I’ll pop up

Based in Cracroft / Cashmere. I come to homes, backyards, and small halls across Ōtautahi Christchurch.
